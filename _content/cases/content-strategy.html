Building a literary audience.

Beyond the words.

Cold Feet Editions was built around the belief that the writing comes first and the products are an extension of it.

I developed a content marketing strategy that places original poetry, literary storytelling and creative expression at the centre of audience discovery. Rather than relying exclusively on product promotion, the strategy aims to build meaningful relationships with readers and create natural pathways from editorial engagement to product exploration.

Audience segmentation, search intent research and content planning informed an integrated approach across organic search, TikTok, Pinterest, email and the website Journal.
