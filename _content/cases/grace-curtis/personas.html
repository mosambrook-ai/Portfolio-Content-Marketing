Different readers. Different worlds.

Understanding the different experiences readers seek from speculative fiction helped shape the proposed author profile and book discovery journey.

These working personas reflect preliminary audience hypotheses. The segments can overlap, and discovery channels are research leads.

Primary audience

LGBTQ+ and queer SFF readers

Books and motivations

  • Relevant novels · Idolfire · Frontier
  • What draws them in · Sapphic slow burn, queer adventure and worlds where queer characters belong.
  • Profile response · Use distinct book hooks for sapphic fantasy and a queer space western.

Discovery and evidence

  • r/QueerSFF and Gay’s The Word are leads to explore.
  • Collect specific reader discussions or bookseller evidence and test whether the two hooks help readers choose.

Core audience

Cosy sci-fi and found-family readers

Books and motivations

  • Relevant novels · Floating Hotel
  • What draws them in · A place they’d like to spend time in, an ensemble cast and a mystery to unravel.
  • Profile response · Lead with the space hotel, then introduce its crew and found family.

Discovery and evidence

  • Goodreads group reads and The StoryGraph are leads to explore.
  • Review specific reader discussions and test the hotel-first hook.

Core audience

Fantasy mystery readers

Books and motivations

  • Relevant novels · Heaven’s Graveyard · Idolfire
  • What draws them in · Buried cities, contested histories and worlds with secrets to uncover.
  • Profile response · Connect both books through Nivela in a short, spoiler-light note.

Discovery and evidence

  • r/Fantasy and Goodreads are leads to explore.
  • Gather specific discussions and check whether the Nivela note helps with book choice.
