Organic search / Launch-stage baseline

The first signs of visibility.

From estimated search demand to observed audience behaviour.

Following the launch of Cold Feet Editions, I established an initial organic search performance baseline to understand how audiences were discovering the studio. Using Google Search Console, I evaluated search visibility, geographic reach, keyword relevance and technical indexation to identify opportunities for future optimisation.

The keyword map estimates monthly demand across related searches. Its combined 17,330 is a planning input, and keyword difficulty is a competition estimate. Neither indicates how often this site appeared.

Search Console impressions count observed appearances in results. Clicks count visits initiated from those results. Sign-ups and orders are separate website outcomes and are outside this snapshot. Use observed queries to test the audience hypotheses and inform future content decisions.
