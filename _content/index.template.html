A little about me

Background

I've always been interested in what words can do. That interest led me to study English Literature at the University of East Anglia before completing an MA in Creative Writing at Manchester Metropolitan University.

My professional background spans independent publishing, bookselling, editorial work and customer-facing management. During my internship at Profile Books, I gained experience across editorial, publicity and marketing, developing an understanding of how books find their readers. Working as a bookseller at Selfridges gave me another perspective on that relationship, from visual merchandising to the conversations that influence purchasing decisions.

More recently, I've developed my digital marketing skills through professional training and the creation of Cold Feet Editions, my independent literary and creative studio. Building a brand from the ground up has allowed me to put content strategy, SEO, social media, email marketing and website development into practice.

I'm particularly interested in the space where creativity meets strategy. I enjoy making things that look beautiful, but I also want to understand why they work, who they're reaching and what could make them better.
