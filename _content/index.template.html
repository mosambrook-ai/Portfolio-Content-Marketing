About / Mollie Sambrook

I've always been interested in what makes people pay attention.

ExperienceAreas of practiceSelected workWritingContactDownload CV

Initially, that interest took me towards literature. I studied English Literature at the University of East Anglia before completing an MA in Creative Writing at Manchester Metropolitan University. I became fascinated by the relationship between language, emotion and the way we make sense of the world through stories.

Working in bookselling and publishing gave me a different perspective on those stories. At Selfridges, I saw how presentation, customer behaviour and cultural trends influence the books people discover. During my internship at Profile Books, I gained experience across editorial, publicity and marketing, developing an understanding of the work that takes place behind a book's publication.

My background in hospitality management also taught me a great deal about people. Managing teams, coordinating events and balancing commercial priorities gave me practical experience of communication, organisation and problem-solving.

Digital marketing felt like a natural extension of these interests. Through professional training and the development of my own independent literary studio, Cold Feet Editions, I've been able to explore how creative ideas can become commercially meaningful through SEO, content strategy, audience research and digital campaigns.

I'm particularly interested in the space where editorial thinking meets marketing. I enjoy creating content that has something to say, understanding who it might resonate with and finding the most effective way to reach them.

Ultimately, I want to create work that doesn't simply capture someone's attention, but gives them a reason to remember it.
